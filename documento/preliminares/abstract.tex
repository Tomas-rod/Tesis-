\begin{abstract}

Organizing a social or corporate event requires contracting services such
as catering, photography, decoration and ambience separately, through
different platforms, social networks and contact channels, without tools
to anticipate problems or compare alternatives before deciding. This
affects both the people who organize events and the venues and suppliers,
who manage their clients and bookings manually or with independent tools.

This work develops a platform that brings together, in a single
environment, a marketplace of event services, management tools for venues
and suppliers, a recommendation system based on weighted rules with an
explainable reason for each suggestion, and a Digital Twin that simulates
changes in guest count, date or budget without altering the actual event.
The system was built on the management prototype of a real case,
Rodríguez Catering, with a multi-tenant architecture, a Laravel server and
a React client. The proposal was validated through a survey of potential
users, three interviews with industry specialists and an automated suite of
410 tests, and its economic viability was assessed under three scenarios.

The results show that integrating the three layers is technically
feasible; that 91.1\,\% of survey respondents would or probably would use
such a platform and rated the recommendation (93.3\,\%) and the simulation
(97.8\,\%) as useful; and that the subscription-plus-commission model is
viable in all three scenarios, with a positive net present value between
USD~33,700 and USD~133,300. The efficiency improvement over fragmented
tools was not measured and is left as future work. The contribution is a
verifiable design that integrates in one platform functions that the
alternatives surveyed offer separately.

\end{abstract}
